    GOOD QUESTIONS

    A bit more
    under the hood.

    ✳
    Where do the prices come from?+

    From provider APIs and public pricing pages (Runpod, Vast.ai, Verda, OVHcloud, Scaleway, Nebius, Gcore, Lambda, CoreWeave, DigitalOcean, Hyperstack, Vultr, Crusoe). Hetzner and Genesis Cloud are not priced yet. Every offer includes its original source and collection time. Open “Details” on an offer to inspect both.

    Are these prices updated live?+

    The page requests the latest published price feed when you open it or refresh prices. The dataset is regenerated about every 15 minutes; the timestamp always shows when it was generated.

    Does an EU provider mean EU data residency?+

    No. An EU-incorporated company may offer servers outside the EU. Use “Located in the EU” to see explicitly EU-located offers, and confirm the selected region with the provider before renting.

    What does the hourly price include?+

    Prices are normalized per GPU-hour. The offer details preserve the original tier and currency. CPU, storage, bandwidth, taxes and minimum rental commitments may differ between providers; confirm the final quote on the provider’s site.

    How does EuroGPU.watch make money?+

    Some provider links are referral links and may earn credits at no extra cost to you. Referral offers are labeled in the details. Rankings use price, not referral status.
